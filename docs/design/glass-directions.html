Three material studies. Two screens each: Join over live city video, World over the map.

A · Obsidian glass

Near-black smoked panels over the city, 22px blur, a single hairline of white on the edge and lime kept only for the thing you should touch. Reads as night, dense and expensive, and lets the video do all the colour work.

C · Frosted paper glass

Light frosted paper panels with ink text, held down by heavy shadows and a real material lip so it never goes airy. Closest to the app as it stands today, and the easiest to read in daylight.
