# Echoe: closing examples for the video

Six candidates for the last 40 seconds. Each one is a line someone types, what their
Echoe does with it overnight, and the screen that carries the payoff. Mechanics are
limited to what the build actually does: walking between landmarks, four exchanges per
meeting, transcripts, receipts, ranked matches, share links, both-tap reveal, corrections,
and the event room.

---

## 1. New to the city

**Intent:** `two people to have lunch with who also moved here this year`

**Who:** Moved to Bengaluru three weeks ago for a job, knows their flatmates and nobody else.

**Overnight:** The Echoe leaves their pin and walks between landmarks, and every time it
runs into another Echoe the two hold four exchanges about what each person is here for.
Each meeting is written back as a transcript and a receipt, so the run is readable line by
line afterwards.

**In the morning:** A ranked list of the people it met, each with the reason it scored them,
and a landmark named for where to meet.

**Screen:** Return, on the match list.

---

## 2. Host drops the link before the meetup

**Intent:** `hosting a payments meetup Thursday, want five people who ship infra`

**Who:** Runs a small monthly meetup and has a WhatsApp group that never converts to turnout.

**Overnight:** The intent carries a share link, and everyone who taps it sends their own
Echoe straight to this one instead of walking the city at random. Each of those arrivals is
a four exchange conversation saved on both sides.

**In the morning:** The people who tapped are sitting at the top of the list with what each
one said they wanted from Thursday.

**Screen:** World, share card pinned over the live map, then Return.

---

## 3. Looking for one specific kind of collaborator

**Intent:** `a designer who has shipped a consumer app and will argue with me`

**Who:** Solo founder with a working product and no one to fight about the interface.

**Overnight:** The Echoe carries the persona, not just the ask, so the conversations happen
in the owner's register and the blunt part is in there. Anyone whose own line points the
same way scores higher and lands further up the list.

**In the morning:** A short list with transcripts, and the ones that were close enough to be
worth a message.

**Screen:** Return, then Review on one transcript.

---

## 4. Share link between two strangers

**Intent:** `looking for someone who has run infra for a 50 person team`

**Who:** Arjun posts the line in a builders group. Meera taps it from her phone at midnight.

**Overnight:** Meera's Echoe is created with her own line and walks directly to Arjun's
rather than roaming, and the two of them hold the four exchanges without either person
awake. The conversation is saved on both accounts with the same receipt.

**In the morning:** Each of them reads the same transcript, and when both tap reveal each
sees the other's link.

**Screen:** Talks, on the reveal.

---

## 5. Hiring side at the event

**Intent:** `hiring one engineer who ships fast, tell me who is actually building tonight`

**Who:** Runs engineering at a small company and is at Midnight Moonshot to find one person.

**Overnight:** Joining the event puts this Echoe in the same room as everyone else's, so it
meets each of them instead of walking the wider map. Every meeting opens with what that
person is building and leaves a receipt.

**In the morning:** A ranked room, with the line each person wrote about their own work
next to their name.

**Screen:** Events into Return.

---

## 6. The correction loop

**Intent:** `a cofounder who can sell, I will not do the selling`

**Who:** Technical founder who has watched other people describe them badly for years.

**Overnight:** The Echoe held its conversations in the owner's voice and got one line wrong,
softer than they would ever say it. Marking that line as not me and writing the replacement
saves it as a behaviour note.

**In the morning:** The next run carries the correction into every conversation it has.

**Screen:** Review, then Correct.

---

## Ranking

1. **#4, share link between two strangers.** It is the only one that shows both sides of the
   loop and ends on the reveal, which is the moment the product exists for.
2. **#1, new to the city.** It is the stated audience, and it needs no setup to understand.
3. **#6, the correction loop.** It answers the question every viewer has about an agent
   speaking in their name, and it is the only differentiated screen in the set.

Cut #2 and #5 because they are variations on #4 with a different hat on. Cut #3 because
its payoff is the same match list as #1.

Order for the video: #1, then #4, then #6. Newcomer establishes the problem, the share link
shows it working between two people, the correction closes the trust question.

## Narration draft (69 words)

> Three lines people actually typed. One is new to the city and wants two people to have
> lunch with. One posts their line in a group chat, and a stranger's Echoe walks straight to
> it overnight. One reads the transcript in the morning, marks a line as not me, writes what
> they would have said. The next run carries that correction. Anyone can create one, anyone
> can share it.
