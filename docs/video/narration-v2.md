## Cut from v1, and why

- The two-phone proof beat and the side-by-side laptop shot. It explains the database to a judge, and the new arc needs those fifteen seconds for what a stranger can do with the app.
- The named participants A and B looking up at each other. The reveal now lands in one clause at 1:11, and the close carries the payoff instead.
- The line about walking two tables over to shake a hand. It ends the story on Jay, and the ending has to end on the viewer.
