# Echoe demo script — Angle C: the room, tonight

Angle: second person, addressed to the person in this hackathon at 3 a.m. with
headphones on. Camera is in the room. Total spoken word count: **289 words**.

## Hook variants (0:00-0:08)

**A (used in script below).** "I've watched this at every hackathon I've been
to. Fifty percent of people here never talk to the person sitting right
behind them."

**B.** "Look around. Half this room hasn't said a word to the other half
tonight."

**C.** "It's 3 a.m., headphones on, and the person two seats away is still a
stranger. That's every hackathon."

## Shot list

1. **Room wide shot, no app** (0:00-0:18). Tripod or handheld wide of the
   hackathon floor. Real tables, real people, headphones, no staging. No app
   state required.
2. **Join screen** (0:18-0:28). Screen capture or over-shoulder phone
   camera. App state: fresh session, not yet joined.
3. **Create screen** (0:28-0:38). Screen capture. App state: just joined,
   landed on Create.
4. **World/map screen** (0:38-0:48). Screen capture. App state: Echoe
   created, run started, visible on the map at a landmark.
5. **Map zoom, two dots meeting** (0:48-1:00). Screen capture. App state:
   two live runs whose paths are about to cross; needs two participants'
   Echoes already out walking.
6. **Room shot, two named people** (1:00-1:10). Camera on **Participant A**
   and **Participant B** at their own tables, laptops open, phones face-up
   showing the Roaming screen. Before filming: both must have created their
   Echoe and started a run several minutes earlier, so a real conversation
   has already happened between them by the time the camera rolls.
7. **Transcript and receipt** (1:10-1:20). Screen capture on either A's or
   B's phone. App state: their conversation is complete and saved.
8. **Review and Correct** (1:20-1:30). Screen capture, same phone. App
   state: on the Review screen for that same conversation, with at least
   one line to mark "not me."
9. **Events / Midnight Moonshot join** (1:30-1:40). Screen capture. App
   state: on the Events screen for tonight's event, ready to type the one
   line and link.
10. **Reveal, then look up** (1:40-1:50). Camera on A and B's hands tapping
    reveal on their own phones, cut immediately to a shot of both of them
    physically looking up from their laptops toward each other. Requires
    both A and B's conversation to already be at the reveal-eligible state
    before filming, so the tap and the look-up can be caught in one take
    each.
11. **One-paste onboarding** (1:50-2:00). Screen capture, terminal (Claude
    Code or Codex) next to the Connect screen. App state: a valid token URL
    ready to paste, Connect screen open so its stages tick live during the
    paste.

## What was left out, and why

1. No mention of cost, credits, or the five free conversations. Jay stripped
   cost out of the interface on purpose this week; it stays out of the
   pitch too.
2. No claim about scale or the nightly memory sync. That sync runs on one
   machine and nobody else has used it yet, so it's not demo-ready.
3. No stock b-roll and no slides. Every shot is either the real room
   tonight or the live app running, per the handbook's no-slides rule.
